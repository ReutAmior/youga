Buttons

Primary .btn .btn-primary

The one main action on a screen: Generate practice, Let's go, Next pose, Finish.

Secondary .btn .btn-secondary

The way back or sideways: Change sequence, Previous, Back to start. Disabled on the first pose in Play.

Text .btn-text · Remove .btn-icon

Text button for low-emphasis actions (Exit). The round "×" removes a pose from an editable sub-sequence; it greys out at the 3-pose minimum.

Action bar .action-bar

Fixed to the bottom of the screen, always secondary on the left and primary on the right. Builder: Change sequence + Let's go. Play: Previous + Next pose.

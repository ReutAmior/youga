# 002 — First users: me, then a public LinkedIn launch in polished phases

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context
Youga is both a product and a public portfolio piece for AI PM interviews. My last LinkedIn post reached about 11K views. I expect a lot of visits but little real use: most visitors will click through in the middle of their workday, curious but not ready to practice.

## Options considered
1. **Private beta only (me and friends):** low risk, but no public feedback and no portfolio value.
2. **Public launch of the full vision at once:** one big moment, but a long wait, and a high risk of shipping something half-finished.
3. **Me first, then public phased releases:** each phase small but polished, with a visible "available now / coming soon" roadmap.

## Decision
Option 3. I test each version on myself first, then share it on LinkedIn. Every phase must be **polished for its scope**: small is fine, messy is not. The app shows what's available now and what's coming next, and it includes a **short in-app demo** for visitors who aren't ready to practice.

## Why
Phased releases give me repeated reasons to post, which builds an audience that wants to see the next step, and they show interviewers how I scope and ship. The demo matches how people actually arrive: they want to *understand* the product in 30 seconds, not do a 15-minute practice at their desk.

## Consequences
- **Two audiences, two jobs:**
  - *Visitors* (most LinkedIn traffic) want to see the idea quickly. That means no forced sign-up, a demo, and the roadmap.
  - *Practitioners* (me and a few others) want to build a habit. They need saved data and reminders.
- **Different success metrics per phase:** for a launch post, success is clicks, demo completion, and feedback comments. For the product, it's people who return the following week. I shouldn't judge the product by launch-day usage.
- **Requires:** a public URL, a "coming soon" section, an in-app demo, a feedback channel, and basic analytics to see what visitors actually do.
- **Quality bar:** smaller scope is the lever. Polish isn't optional.
- **Revisit if:** posts bring in real practitioners rather than browsers. Then the product needs to grow faster than the posting schedule.
